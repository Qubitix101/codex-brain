# Step 1 - Clarify Intent

Confirm:

- goal
- target user
- visible outcome
- scope
- out of scope
- verification expectation

Check escalation triggers:

- auth or user data
- payments
- multi-tenancy
- GDPR or regulated domain
- AI-core behavior
- enterprise buyer
- major architecture/database change
- frontend without Design DNA

If any trigger applies, stop Quick Flow and route to Standard or Full.
