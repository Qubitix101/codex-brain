# Validation Framework

Validation asks whether the project deserves investment.

## Light

Answer:

- What are we building?
- Who is it for?
- What problem does it solve?
- What does success look like?

## Standard

Add:

- alternatives
- user pain
- business or operational value
- key risks
- initial go/no-go

## Full

Add:

- market size
- moat
- timing
- competition
- unit economics
- founder/project fit
- pre-mortem
- kill criteria

## Output

```text
GO
CONDITIONAL GO
PIVOT
NO-GO
```

Do not proceed to deep planning if the project is a no-go.
